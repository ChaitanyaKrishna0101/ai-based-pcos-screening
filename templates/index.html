What is PCOS, really?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome is a common hormonal condition where the ovaries produce slightly more androgens (male-type hormones) than usual. It can affect periods, skin, hair growth, weight, and fertility — but it looks different in every person, and most cases are very manageable once identified.

Irregular cycles

Longer gaps between periods are one of the most common early signs.

⚖️

Weight shifts

Insulin resistance can make weight harder to manage for some people.

✨

Skin & hair changes

Acne, oily skin, or extra facial/body hair can be androgen-related signs.

🧠

It's manageable

Diet, movement, and — when needed — medical care lead to good outcomes.
